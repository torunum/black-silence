# Impact: hit feel, the kick, and gore

The owner, 2026-10-08:

> I want the character models developed further, in the style. I want
> decapitation and leg and arm severing to be better — more gore. I want the
> kick animation to be better. The hit feel should be better.

Three plans, in this order: **this one (hit feel + kick, then gore)**, then
enemy art. Enemy sprite redraw is its own plan after this.

## Global Constraints

- `reference/sonsurum.html` is never edited.
- **No gameplay value changes** — damage, fire rate, kick cooldown, kick hit
  timing (the 110 ms resolve), enemy HP and speeds stay. Feel is presentation:
  reactions, timing of visuals and sound, camera, particles. Where a feel
  change *must* touch simulation (knockback distance, stagger time,
  hit-stop), argue it, keep it small, and record it.
- **Trace fixtures**: presentation that adds scene objects or draws from the
  seeded `Math.random` moves fixtures. Prefer deterministic variation
  (grid-hash, a local PRNG, a counter) so the seeded stream is untouched. If
  a fixture moves, follow its header's procedure: prove field by field what
  moved and why, write the analysis, regenerate. The boss trace's guard
  window is narrow — anything that shifts the seeded stream moves the fight.
- Gore lands on the local floor (`floorHeightAt` / `e.fy`) — the fix just
  merged; keep it.
- Pooled, bounded, no per-frame allocation in hot paths.
- No `src/` file over 400 lines; no import cycles; `npm test` green with the
  exit code captured; Write tool for scripts; never `git checkout --`;
  commit and push early; nothing untracked; **no `Co-Authored-By`**.

## Task 1 — hit feel

Make every hit land. Measure what exists first (`hitStop`, `shake`/trauma,
recoil, `pain`, `stun`, `kbRes`, the hit sounds, the muzzle flash) and then:

- **The enemy reacts:** a white hit-flash on the sprite for a frame or two; a
  flinch (the existing pain frame, held correctly; a small push back along the
  shot); heavier weapons stagger.
- **The player feels it:** hit-stop scaled by weapon and by kill vs hit;
  a directional camera punch on heavy hits and kills; a crisp hit sound
  layered over the report, distinct for flesh / armour / headshot / kill.
- **Confirmation:** a short, tasteful hit marker at the crosshair (and a
  distinct kill marker), with headshots distinct.
- Tune per weapon so the shotgun and BMG feel enormous and the nail cannon
  feels like a stream, not 20 separate jolts.

## Task 2 — the kick

The kick already has a leg in the viewmodel rig. Make it *connect*:

- a sharper wind-up and a harder snap at the 110 ms hit;
- on contact: hit-stop, a heavy camera punch, boot-impact dust and blood,
  a meaty layered sound;
- the kicked enemy is **launched** — lifted, tumbling, sliding, slamming into
  walls with a splat (much of this exists: `flung`, `kx/kz`, the wall splat);
  make it read big;
- a miss whiffs: a whoosh and a little overreach;
- a kicked head, prop or barrel reacts properly.

## Task 3 — gore

Make dismemberment and decapitation spectacular, in the game's pixel style:

- **Decapitation:** the head flies with a blood trail and spins; the neck
  stump fountains for a moment (pulses of spray); the body stumbles a step
  and collapses; blood splashes the nearest wall and the floor.
- **Limbs:** severed arms and legs fly with trails and land with a splat,
  showing bone and meat at the stump end; the stump spurts; an enemy that
  loses a leg drops and crawls (the sprite variants exist —
  `refreshSeverSprite`).
- **Overkill:** big damage gibs the whole body into meat, bone and organ
  chunks, with a blood burst and mist.
- **Persistence:** blood decals that stay (floor pools, wall splatter in the
  shot's direction, smears where a body slid); corpses and parts stay within a
  budget.
- All of it pooled and capped; levels-feel-full's decor and the light budget
  untouched.

## Definition of done

Each task: before/after frame strips in the report, a headless check of
the timing (hit-stop, marker, kick snap at 110 ms), mutations that bite,
fixtures handled per procedure, `npm test` green, merged with a build.
