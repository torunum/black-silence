# Blood reference study — 2 November 2026

This pass starts from GitHub main `567328539d2719f8a23a5951ef89553b5836484b`. Blood is a design reference, not a source of copied textures, sprites, audio or level layouts. The existing Black Silence hand/weapon boards and gothic hell target remain the controlling aesthetic references.

## Primary evidence actually inspected

The [official Atari Blood: Fresh Supply page](https://atari.com/products/blood-fresh-supply) identifies the included original Blood and its expansions. Its screenshots were visually inspected in the browser on 2 November; the image retrieval tool could not render the CDN images, but the browser did. No downloaded Blood asset enters the game. Fresh Supply's modern rendering options should not be confused with a different underlying art direction.

- [Official screenshot 02](https://atari.com/cdn/shop/files/blood_ss_02_1920x1080_eee78133-5b2a-4f9f-9881-674f4dc1cd73.jpg?v=1661796603): dark brick chamber, raised side ledges, substantial columns, horizontal skull decoration, local bright fire against a restrained shell. The projecting ledges and bands articulate room height without filling the central route with props.
- [Official screenshot 03](https://atari.com/cdn/shop/files/blood_ss_03_1920x1080_57855ce3-ec13-4f8c-b16e-0f6c7e114278.jpg?v=1661796627): grey masonry on the left, red brick and timber arcade on the right; raised sidewalk, canopy and dark recessed passages give simple geometry depth. The broad cobbled foreground remains readable around enemies. A shotgun reload still shows two separate dark breeches with thin metal rims, red/brass spent shells above, and fresh rounds held at lower right. This is evidence of clear mechanical silhouettes, not of animation duration or event timing.
- [Official screenshot 04](https://atari.com/cdn/shop/files/blood_ss_04_1920x1080_a2ea1ff3-e93e-43ac-b024-e9fcdebaeb86.jpg?v=1661796663): a wood door framed by dark red/black timber, pale wall fields, a lateral foreground crate, and local flame. Different material regions make the doorway readable. The crate adds a foreground layer while leaving the central approach clear.

## Adaptation decisions (our interpretation)

1. Give prologue spaces distinct material regions and recognisable architectural roles. A recessed threshold, low wall plinth, timber or metal framing and a few edge props do more than distributing the same small decoration everywhere.
2. Keep the route and enemy silhouette legible. Put dense detail around the room edges and landmarks, with deliberately quieter walking surfaces. Check this from player height and during a real route, not only from an elevated camera.
3. Use light to separate a focal feature from its surrounding mass. The hell scene should retain a cooler/darker stone shell around warm lava and fire; adding orange illumination everywhere would erase that contrast.
4. For the shotgun, make the hinge, two breech openings, extracted cases and inserted rounds separately readable throughout reload. Preserve this game's approved hand identity and loading-hand arrangement. Do not claim to reproduce Blood's cadence from a screenshot.
5. Preserve the existing game's weapon detail and gothic language. Blood supports clarity, material contrast and purposeful environmental storytelling; it does not justify reducing the approved weapon art to its 1997 pixel density or copying its rooms.

## Evaluation boundary

These observations do not prove Blood's exact collision layout, lighting implementation, reload timing or performance. No source-code or timing claims are made. Each Black Silence candidate must still pass independent native-frame comparison, route/collision checks and measured frame-time checks. More decoration alone is not accepted as an improvement.
